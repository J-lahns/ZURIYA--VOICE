Before history remembered them,
there was a story.

Across the ancient lands of Africa, stories traveled farther than armies.

They were carried by elders, whispered beside fires, passed from one generation to another.

Stories of kings who ruled kingdoms of gold.

Warriors who stood against impossible odds.

Spirits who guarded sacred rivers.

And cities whose greatness has almost disappeared from memory.

But among these stories was one that refused to die.

A story about a king.

A king who was said to have conquered death itself.

His people called him immortal.

But immortality was never his gift.

It was his punishment.

And this...

is where his story begins.
